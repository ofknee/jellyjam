Questions

FAQ

Answers to common questions.

What is a game jam? ↓

A game jam is an event where you build a game from scratch in a short period of time. It's about creativity, learning, and having fun.

Am I eligible to participate? ↓

If you're a teenager (13–18), you're in. No prior experience needed.

What if I don't have any experience? ↓

That's totally fine. There'll be workshops and mentors throughout the day to help you get unstuck.

Is this free? ↓

Yes, everything is free — venue, food, workshops, prizes. Just show up.

What do I need to bring? ↓

A laptop and a charger. We'll handle the rest.
