Originality

Questions

FAQ

Answers to common questions.

What is a hackathon? ↓

A hackathon is a coding event where you build a program from scratch in a short period of time (in our case, 12 hours).

Am I eligible to participate? ↓

All teenagers (13–18) are eligible.

What if I don't have any experience? ↓

That's totally fine! There'll be workshops and mentors throughout the day to help you get unstuck.

Is this free? ↓

Yes, everything is free. Just show up!

What do I need to bring? ↓

A laptop, a charger, and some form of ID.

What kind of projects are allowed? ↓

Any kind of project, as long as it's on theme! We encourage creativity and innovation.

Can I work with others? ↓

Yes! You can form teams of up to 3 people and work together on your projects.
